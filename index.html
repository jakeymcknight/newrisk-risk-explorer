Which county are you planning for?

Select your county to see how its health facilities are exposed to extreme weather, where they are most likely to fail, and what to fix first. Assessments run on a 6–12 month cycle, timed to land before annual workplans are drafted.

⚠ Demonstration data. Facility names, sub-counties, KEPH levels and approximate coordinates are real. Every assessment score, staffing figure and utility reading is synthetic — generated to exercise the tool ahead of fieldwork, not collected from facilities.

NEWRISK — A Novel Extreme Weather Risk Insurance System for Kenya. Protecting vulnerable populations in Kenya from the effects of extreme weather by working with them to create meaningful local plans backed by sustainable financial solutions.

This project is funded by the National Institute for Health and Care Research (NIHR). Delivered with KEMRI–Wellcome Trust, the University of Oxford and the Kenya Red Cross Society. Kenyan stakeholders direct and lead all components of the project.
